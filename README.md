\## Key Findings



\### Restaurant Type Analysis



\* Quick Bites dominated the market with \*\*19,132 restaurants\*\*, followed by Casual Dining with \*\*10,330 restaurants\*\*.

\* However, premium categories achieved significantly higher ratings. Microbrewery \& Casual Dining restaurants recorded an average rating of \*\*4.37\*\*, compared to \*\*3.55\*\* for Quick Bites.



\### Online Order Analysis



\* Restaurants accepting online orders achieved an average rating of \*\*3.72\*\*, compared to \*\*3.66\*\* for restaurants without online ordering.

\* Online-order restaurants also generated \*\*9.34 million votes\*\*, significantly higher than the \*\*5.31 million votes\*\* received by restaurants without online ordering.



\### Cuisine Analysis



\* North Indian cuisine was the most common category with \*\*2,158 restaurants\*\*, but its average rating was only \*\*3.59\*\*.

\* In contrast, Cafe, American, Burger \& Steak restaurants achieved an average rating of \*\*4.60\*\* and an average of \*\*7,001 votes per restaurant\*\*, indicating strong customer satisfaction and engagement.



\### Location Analysis



\* BTM contained the highest number of restaurants (\*\*2,610\*\*), followed by Koramangala 7th Block (\*\*2,390\*\*).

\* MG Road, Church Street, and Brigade Road achieved the highest average ratings among major restaurant clusters, each recording an average rating of \*\*3.80\*\*.



\### Cost vs Rating Analysis



\* Budget restaurants priced around \*\*₹100–₹300 for two people\*\* maintained average ratings between \*\*3.56 and 3.57\*\*.

\* Ratings increased noticeably beyond the \*\*₹600–₹700\*\* price range.

\* Restaurants priced at \*\*₹1,100–₹1,200\*\* achieved average ratings of approximately \*\*4.05\*\*, indicating a positive relationship between pricing and customer ratings.



\### Market Saturation Analysis



\* JP Nagar (\*\*1,649 restaurants, 3.63 rating\*\*) and Jayanagar (\*\*1,928 restaurants, 3.69 rating\*\*) showed signs of market saturation due to high restaurant density combined with relatively lower customer ratings.



\### Business Model Analysis



\* Restaurants offering table booking achieved the highest ratings.

\* Restaurants with \*\*Table Booking = Yes\*\* and \*\*Online Order = No\*\* recorded the highest average rating of \*\*4.16\*\*.

\* Restaurants without both services recorded the lowest average rating of \*\*3.55\*\*.

\* This suggests table reservation availability has a stronger association with customer ratings than online ordering.
